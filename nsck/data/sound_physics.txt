The Physics of Sound by Dr. Hz

Sound is a form of energy.
Sound travels as a wave of vibrations through a medium.
A medium can be air, water, or solid crystal.

Frequency is the speed of the vibration.
High frequency creates a high pitch.
Low frequency creates a low pitch.
Resonance is a phenomenon where an external vibration matches natural frequency.
Resonance amplifies the sound.

Harmonics are multiples of a base frequency.
Music is a collection of ordered harmonics.
Noise is a collection of disordered vibrations.
