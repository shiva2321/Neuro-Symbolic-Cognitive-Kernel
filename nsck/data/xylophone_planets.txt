The Ecosystem of Xylophone Planets by Asta

Xylophone planets are rare celestial bodies made entirely of singing crystal.
Singing crystal is a unique mineral that vibrates when exposed to starlight.
These vibrations produce a melodic resonance that echoes across the cosmos.

Resonance attracts space_whales.
Space_whales are gentle giants that feed on pure sound.
When space_whales feed, they release star_dust.
Star_dust settles on the crystal surface and causes new crystals to grow.

There are different types of crystal formations.
Blue_shards are a type of singing crystal that hums at a low frequency.
Red_spires are a type of singing crystal that chimes at a high frequency.

The Harmonium is a dangerous phenomenon.
The Harmonium is caused by discordant vibrations.
Discordant vibrations crack the crystal surface.
If the surface cracks, the resonance stops.
Without resonance, the space_whales leave.
Without space_whales, the Xylophone planet slowly fades into silence.

Explorers use a tuning_fork to navigate these worlds.
A tuning_fork produces a reference tone.
This tone helps explorers avoid areas with discordant vibrations.
