# Societal Hypervectors: Multi-scale Hierarchical Knowledge Representation in Neuro-Symbolic Systems

**Abstract**:
Traditional vector spaces for knowledge representation often suffer from "semantic smearing" where high-dimensional overlaps lose structural nuance. We propose **Societal Hypervectors**, a framework where hypervectors are treated as autonomous entities within a hierarchical social world. By applying **Spectral Renormalization** and **Hodge Laplacians**, we demonstrate emergent city-scale structures that mirror human societal organization, enabling robust, multi-scale reasoning in neuro-symbolic cognitive kernels.

The transition from static semantic memory to "Living Memory" requires a shift in how we observe and manage hypervector interactions. In NSCK V5, we introduce the concept of the **Societal Knowledge World**.

## 3. Implementation

### 3.1 Spectral Renormalization
To handle millions of concepts, we coarse-grain the graph by identifying dominant spectral modes. This allows the system to reason about "Physics" as a single entity without losing the underlying complexity of "Quantum Mechanics" citizens.

### 3.2 Percolation Monitoring
Domains emerge not by manual assignment, but by autonomous percolation. When a cluster of concepts crosses a critical similarity threshold, the `PercolationMonitor` formalizes it into a new jurisdictional unit.
