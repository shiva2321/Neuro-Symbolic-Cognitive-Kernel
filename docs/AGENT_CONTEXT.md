## What We're Building

A hybrid AI combining:
1. **SNN (Spiking Neural Network)** - Fast intuition, ternary quantized
2. **VSA (Vector Symbolic Architecture)** - Symbolic reasoning, 10k-bit hypervectors
3. **Metacognition** - Arbitrates between neural and symbolic
